*Foundation vs Growth – Website Packages*
_Groundwork Studios_

*🟢 Foundation* – get found & get contacted
• Home, About, Portfolio, Reviews & Contact pages
• A page for every service you offer
• Before & after photos of your work
• Contact form + click-to-call
• FAQ section
• 10 pages, fully mobile-friendly
👉 See it live: https://danielhussain266-lang.github.io/demo-driveways/tier1/

*🔵 Growth* – turns visitors into leads on its own
Everything in Foundation, plus:
• Instant quote/estimate calculator
• Drag-to-reveal before & after slider
• Step-by-step booking form (not just one big form)
• Live chat-style enquiry box
• Blog, case studies, area-specific pages & team bios
• Video intro on the homepage
• 36 pages – more pages Google can show people
👉 See it live: https://danielhussain266-lang.github.io/demo-driveways/

*✨ Also possible on Growth (add-ons, built to order)*
• Online booking with calendar & auto-reminders
• Automatic review collection & display
• Extra location pages for each area you cover
• Seasonal offers/promo banners
• Email sign-up & newsletter capture
• Live price list or stock/availability display
• Multiple languages
• Google Maps & business profile integration

*Bottom line:* Foundation makes you look professional and easy to contact. Growth makes the website do the selling and the answering — even when you're not at your phone.
