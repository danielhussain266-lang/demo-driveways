What We Found

Five gaps costing you enquiries today

The full technical audit covered all 12 pages of the site, your Google Business Profile, and Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Here's what matters most for booking volume.

01

No tracking installed anywhere on the site. There's no way to know which enquiries come from Google, Facebook, or Instagram — or whether marketing spend is working at all.

02

No pricing on either package page. Couples can't budget-check themselves before enquiring, so they either don't enquire or enquire unqualified.

03

Your Instagram bio link — seen by 41.4K followers — leads to a 13-word dead end with no form, phone number, or pricing.

04

Negative reviews go unanswered. None of your reviews at 3 stars or below have a reply, while your 5-star reviews are answered warmly. Every prospect reading reviews sees the complaints sitting unaddressed.

05

Links shared in WhatsApp or Facebook groups show no image or title — just a bare grey link, in a market that runs on exactly that kind of sharing.

The Plan

Two workstreams, built to fix what we found

The website work closes the leaks. The social management turns your existing 120K-follower audience into a channel we can actually measure and grow.
